Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI predict wait times?
Our machine learning models (scikit-learn RandomForestRegressor) analyze historical queuing rates, service speeds, day-of-week variables, and live check-in volumes to dynamically forecast waiting durations.
Is it easy to integrate with local registration systems?
Yes! We provide robust REST APIs and webhook support to plug SmartQueueAI directly into your existing registration, ticketing, or CRM software databases.
